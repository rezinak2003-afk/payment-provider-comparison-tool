Formula

SUMPRODUCT(ratings, weights) / 100

Each rating is 1–5. The weights total 100%. Higher integration-effort ratings mean an easier integration, so direction is consistent.

How it would be used in operations

1 · Define fit

Set the target countries, payment methods, volumes, settlement needs, and technical constraints.

2 · Gather evidence

Request written pricing, uptime reports, SLA terms, technical docs, security evidence, and licensing/compliance details.

3 · Score consistently

Rate each provider against one rubric and record the evidence source, owner, and capture date.

4 · Track decisions

Assign follow-ups, deadlines, dependencies, blockers, and decision owners. Escalate gaps rather than guessing.

Scope and data integrity. All provider names, values, and ratings in this demo are synthetic. The score is a first-pass prioritisation aid only, not a real-world vendor ranking or compliance assessment. Final selection requires verified provider evidence and review from Engineering, Security, Risk, Compliance, Legal, Finance, and business stakeholders as applicable.
